About

Songs that lift people up. Businesses that open doors.

Sammystave is a Nigerian gospel artist, songwriter and music producer. He writes and produces across many styles, and Afro gospel is one of them.

He released his first song, “You Reign,” in March 2018, and “Amazing Grace” followed in September 2018. He has kept writing, producing and building since, and he also designs and builds websites.

He is the founder of Spelbum, Stave Industries Inc. and Rap, Gospel and Praise Nation (RGPN). His goal is to make music that lifts people up and to build businesses that give Nigerian creators and young people real opportunities.
